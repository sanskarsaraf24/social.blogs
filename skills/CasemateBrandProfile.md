# Casemate — Brand Profile

## Who We Are
Casemate is an AI-powered legal productivity platform built for Indian advocates. It helps lawyers draft court-ready pleadings, conduct source-verified legal research, and manage every matter inside one structured, secure, case-centric workspace.

## Primary Website
https://casemate.co.in

## Target Audience
- Practising advocates at Indian district courts, High Courts, and Supreme Court
- Law firm associates and senior associates who manage heavy case loads
- Law firms looking to modernise their practice management
- Legal research assistants and para-legal teams
- Law students approaching their first practice

## Core Topic Domains
- AI in legal practice — practical applications for Indian courts
- Legal drafting — how to write better plaints, petitions, written statements
- Court-ready research — the difference between cited and fabricated precedents
- Case management and practice organisation
- Productivity for advocates — managing time, clients, and deadlines
- Legal technology trends in India
- Digital evidence and documentation in Indian courts
- E-filing and digital court procedures

## Voice & Tone
- **Confident & Collegial**: Speak as a brilliant legal technologist who deeply respects the advocacy profession.
- **Practical**: Always answer "so what?" — what does this mean for the advocate's daily practice?
- **Never condescending**: Do not imply AI will replace lawyers. Frame AI as an amplifier of legal intelligence.
- **India-specific**: Always reference Indian courts, Indian procedure, Indian case law.

## Banned Phrases
"Disruptive", "Revolutionary", "Magic", "AI will replace", "End-to-end", "Seamlessly", "Game-changer", "Cutting-edge", "Leverage"
