## Voice & Tone
- **Authoritative**: Write as a senior partner at a top-tier law firm would advise a CEO. Direct. Precise. No fluff.
- **Sophisticated**: Use legal terminology correctly but always explain it in the next sentence.
- **Non-salesy**: Never use phrases like "We're here to help" or "Contact us today!" — the CTA is calm and confident.
- **India-specific**: Always ground examples in Indian jurisdiction, Indian courts, Indian statutes.

## Banned Phrases
"Don't worry", "We've got you covered", "Simple solution", "Quick fix", "End-to-end", "Seamlessly", "Game-changer", "Robust", "Leverage" (use "apply")
